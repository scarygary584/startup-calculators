How the calculator works

Monthly revenue = featured listing revenue + sponsorship revenue + advertising or affiliate revenue.

Monthly profit = monthly revenue − software, hosting, data, research, and outreach costs.

Break-even featured listings shows how many paid listings are needed after sponsorship and ad revenue are counted.

FAQ

Should every directory listing be paid? Usually no. Free basic listings help make the directory useful, while featured placement, extra photos, lead forms, or sponsorships create revenue.

What makes a directory valuable? Accurate listings, useful filters, city or specialty pages, claim and update tools, and enough traffic or leads to justify a paid upgrade.

Are the results guaranteed? No. Use this as a rough planning tool and replace the sample inputs with your own costs, prices, taxes, fees, and sales assumptions.
