How the calculator works

Revenue per event = guests × price per guest.

Profit per event = revenue − food − labor − rentals − delivery.

Monthly profit = profit per event × events per month − monthly overhead.

FAQ

What should catering food cost include? Include ingredients, beverages, garnishes, waste, tasting portions, and packaging that changes with guest count.

Should labor include the owner? Yes. Include a fair cost for your own prep, delivery, setup, service, and cleanup time.

Are the results guaranteed? No. Use this as a rough planning tool and replace the sample inputs with your own costs, prices, taxes, fees, permits, and local requirements.
