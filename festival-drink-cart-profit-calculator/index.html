How the calculator works

Event revenue = event days × customers per day × average sale.

Variable cost includes product cost and card-processing fees.

Event profit = revenue − variable costs − booth fee − labor − permits and travel.

FAQ

What drinks fit this calculator? Use it for lemonade, agua fresca, iced tea, flavored soda, coffee, mocktails, energy drinks, snow cones, or any cart with a per-sale product cost.

Why include break-even drinks? It shows how many sales are needed before the cart begins paying you instead of merely covering the event.

Are the results guaranteed? No. Use this as a rough planning tool and replace the sample inputs with your own costs, prices, taxes, fees, permits, and local requirements.
