How to use the calculators

  1. Choose a calculator from the menu.
  2. Replace the example numbers with your prices, costs and workload. Results update as you change the inputs.
  3. Compare a slow month, a typical month and a busy month.

Defaults are examples, not promises of demand, current local prices or earnings. Each calculator subtracts only the expenses shown in its inputs. Revenue is sales before expenses; profit and owner earnings depend on which costs are included. Add missing costs and count each expense once.

Include your time, paid helpers, travel, insurance, equipment upkeep, overhead, taxes and financing as applicable. Owner earnings can include payment for doing the work. Annual estimates assume the same activity continues. N/A means a ratio or payback cannot be calculated with those inputs.

These are planning estimates, not quotes or financial, tax, legal or licensing advice.
