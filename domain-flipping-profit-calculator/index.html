How the calculator works

Net profit per sale = sale price − marketplace commission − payment fees − purchase price − holding renewals.

Break-even sale price includes all costs and percentage-based selling fees.

Expected portfolio profit applies the sell-through rate to the number of domains, then subtracts annual renewals on the unsold inventory.

FAQ

Is an automated domain appraisal a real offer? No. An appraisal is only an estimate. The domain is worth what a real buyer will actually pay.

Why use a sell-through rate? Most domain portfolios sell only a small percentage of names each year, so expected value is more useful than assuming every name sells.

Are the results guaranteed? No. Use this as a rough planning tool and replace the sample inputs with your own costs, prices, taxes, fees, and marketplace terms.
